    body: |-
      I was traveling over the weekend so I decided to set up a book loom so that my work would be portable. Here is a list of all the materials I brought with me to weave the circuit:

      - Book loom
      - Silicone coated magnet wire
      - Copper tape
      - DIY connectors from previous lab
      - A few different yarn colors
      - 9V battery
      - White LED
      - 330 Ohm resistor

      I wanted the warps to stay in place so I cut slots into the top and bottom of the book cover to hold the yarn.

    text: Circuit
  - type: paragraph
    body: For the circuit I needed to connect battery(+) ---> resistor ---> magnet wire ---> LED ---> battery(-), but I'm leaving the connection between the LED and battery(-) open so that the circuit can be closed by folding the two sides together or clipping connectors between the two ends. I knew that I wanted to use the battery, resistor, LED, and wiring as decorative elements, but the ends of the circuits and connections (using copper tape to connect everything) need to be hidden. The biggest challenge here is not being able to see the back of the cloth as I'm weaving the wires through. I know that it will look messy on the back, but I'm more concerned with the front, since the two sides will fold together.
